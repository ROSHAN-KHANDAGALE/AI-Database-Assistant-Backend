# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Offer constructive feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Help keep discussions focused, inclusive, and useful.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Insults, deliberate disruption, or abusive language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or other conduct that makes participation unsafe.

## Reporting

Report behavior that violates this code to the project maintainers through a private channel. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Maintainers should review reports promptly, fairly, and confidentially, and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-related communications.
